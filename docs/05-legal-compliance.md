# LastSeat India: Legal and Compliance Checklist

This is a working checklist, not legal advice. Have an Indian lawyer review it before launch.

- Operate as a travel agent / aggregator under the airline's agreement; confirm required registrations
- Refund and cancellation terms per DGCA passenger rules
- Payment handling via a licensed gateway; GST on pass
- Clear disclosure that seats are unguaranteed at purchase time
- Passenger data handled under DPDP Act, 2023

## Always
- Privacy policy and terms of service before collecting any real user data.
- A grievance officer and a data-deletion path for users.
- Entity setup (e.g. private limited company), GST registration when thresholds apply.
